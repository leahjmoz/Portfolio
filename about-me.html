Hi! I'm Leah Mozeleski and I'm a recent graduate of Virginia Commonwealth University with a BFA in Graphic Design and a minor in Marketing.

I got into design the same way people get into their favorite sports team, slowly, then all at once. It started small, noticing things I would tweak on a game day graphic, then reimagining a team's uniform design, just because I thought I could make them a little better.

What I love most about this work is taking something people already care about, whether it's a team, an artist, or a moment, and giving it a visual voice that makes people stop and look. That same instinct carries over when the subject is less flashy too. I enjoy the challenge of taking dense information or ideas and turning them into graphics that are clear and easy to follow. I'm comfortable across the Adobe Creative Suite, and I show up ready to learn quickly, take feedback well, and put in the extra hours on the details.
